Bharat Niti Lipi: publication flow for Acts and subordinate legislation

How a signed law moves from Parliament or a ministry to the e-Gazette, through parliamentary scrutiny, to public verification. Each step links to its legal source.

Draft for discussion, 10 November 2026.

  • Acts track
  • Subordinate legislation track
  • Both tracks
  • Branch or condition
  • ProposalDesign choice, not current law

Scope: from passage by both Houses (Manual 9.21) and from the grant of rule-making power (Manual 11.10). Drafting and passage (Manual 9.1 to 9.20) come before this flow. “Manual” is the Manual of Parliamentary Procedures in the Government of India.

Issuance: two parallel tracks

A. Acts of Parliament

A1Bill passed by both Houses

Copy to the Legislative Department for scrutiny and correction of patent errors.

  • Manual 9.19, 9.21(a)

A2Reprint and second scrutiny

Azure laid paper, “as passed by the Houses of Parliament”. Scrutinised again. In BNL, this is the bill file to be signed.

  • Manual 9.21(b)

A3Chairman or Speaker signs (signature 1)

Two copies. Money Bill: the Speaker’s certificate is endorsed.

  • Manual 9.21(c)
  • Lok Sabha Rule 128
  • Rajya Sabha at Work, ch. 21
  • Constitution, Art. 110(4)

A4Sent to the President through the Legislative Department

The Legislative Department verifies signature 1 and forwards the file unchanged.

  • Manual 9.21(c)
  • G.S.R. 660(E), r.10

A5President decides

Assent: signature 2, a counter signature over signature 1. The assent date is the date the Bill becomes an Act.

  • Constitution, Art. 111
  • G.S.R. 660(E), r.9(5)
  • Manual 9.21(e)
Withhold: the flow stops. Return (not a Money Bill): the Houses reconsider, then back to A1.

A6Assent date notified

To the administrative ministry and the Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs.

  • Manual 9.21(e)

A7Custody of the signed Act

The Legislative Department, the Secretariat and the President’s Secretariat each keep a copy. In BNL, each archives the same signed file.

  • Manual 9.21(e)

A8BNL Act made and signed (signature 3)

Act number, assent date and a link to the signed bill. Submitted for the Gazette of India Extraordinary.

  • Manual 9.22(a)
  • G.S.R. 660(E), r.9

B. Subordinate legislation

B1Rule-making power in force

Starts after D2. The ministry identifies the sections that give the power and keeps a register. Rules are due in 6 months, or the Committee on Subordinate Legislation gives an extension of up to 3 months at a time.

  • Manual 11.10, 11.11, 11.3.1, 11.3.2

B2Ministry frames draft rules in BNL

Each rule links to the section that gives the power. Checklist: consultations done, competent approval obtained, gazette references of the principal rules added.

  • Manual 11.1.2, 11.1.3
  • BNL deck: delegated legislation linkage
The Act does not require previous publication: go to B5.

B3Draft published for objections

Through C1 to C9, marked as a draft. Objections within 30 days. Registered letters or press publication if needed.

  • Manual 11.2(a) to (c)
  • 2014 pre-legislative consultation policy

B4Objections considered

After 30 clear days. Final rules in 3 months (few objections) or 6 months (many). The preamble cites the draft.

  • Manual 11.2(d), (e), 11.1.3(iii)

B5Legislative Department vets

From the constitutional, legal and drafting points of view. ProposalA vetting signature, checked at C3. Decision pending.

  • Manual 11.1.2, 11.2(f)
  • Committee on Subordinate Legislation on vetting

B6Translation

Hindi version prepared. English and Hindi copies are needed for laying.

  • Manual 11.11, 11.5.2

B7Ministry LPO signs and submits

DSC or eSign. The ministry is responsible for submitting the notification.

  • Manual 11.4, 11.5.4(i)
  • G.S.R. 660(E): DSC
  • G.S.R. 446(E): eSign
A8 and B7 both go to C1

C. Publication by the Custodian (both tracks)

C1Intake API

Extends the eGazette e-Publish module. Gazette notifications are e-published only, under G.S.R. 746(E) of 30 September 2015.

  • Manual 11.5.3
  • eGazette

C2Authenticate submitter

The submitting organisation proves its identity with an organisational certificate.

  • CCA Interoperability Guidelines

C3Validate file and signatures

BNL schema, each signature, certificate status, signer authorised on the signing date, and an XSLT that renders all of the BNL file, including meta.

  • G.S.R. 660(E), r.9(6), r.10, r.11
Invalid: returned to the sender with the reason, then back to A8 or B7.

C4Lock (freeze)

No change after this point.

  • EU Official Journal Signature Policy, s.3.1.1.3 (precedent)

C5Number and wrap

G.S.R. or S.O. number, Part and Section of the Gazette. Two amendments to the same rules in one issue keep their order. Wrapped in an AKN officialGazette issue.

  • Manual 11.5.2(c), 11.7.5
  • AKN officialGazette
  • AKN 3.1 namespace

C6Organisation seal and timestamp

The Controller of Publications, as Custodian, seals with a Document Signer certificate. Assumption: chosen for high volume and to avoid dependence on individual officers.

  • Custodian: Controller of Publications
  • CCA-ESAIG: Document Signer
  • G.S.R. 660(E), r.11: RFC 3161

C7Append hash to public log

ProposalAn append-only log of every publication hash.

  • RFC 9162, Certificate Transparency (model)

C8Publish in the e-Gazette

Satisfies the requirement to publish in the Official Gazette. Downloaded copies count as electronic versions. Courts presume an electronic Gazette genuine.

  • IT Act, s.4 and s.8
  • Manual 11.5.4(ii)
  • Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, s.81

C9Event and archive

A publication event goes to all subscribers. The file, XSLT, signatures and timestamps are archived together.

  • RFC 6283, XMLERS
  • G.S.R. 660(E), r.9(7)
Split by track

D. After publication: steps for each track

Acts

D1State Gazettes

Copies go to all State Governments for their Official Gazettes. Sale copies are printed. ProposalThe Fetch API serves this need.

  • Manual 9.22(b), (c)

D2Commencement

On the date of assent, unless the Act states otherwise. Or on dates the Government appoints by notification, sometimes section by section. A commencement notification goes through B7 and C.

  • General Clauses Act, s.5
  • Example: S.O. 582(E)
  • PRS note on notification of Acts
Power in force: track B starts at B1 for the rules under this Act.

Subordinate legislation

D3Parliament Secretariats notified

Each statutory order goes to the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha Secretariats at the time of notification. Today by email; in BNL by the publication event.

  • Manual 11.5.4(iii)
  • Committee on Subordinate Legislation, 18th report

D4Laid before Parliament

Within 15 days (30 for a State under President’s rule), in English and Hindi, with a statement. Re-laid if the period is not complete. Laid again after dissolution of the Lok Sabha.

  • Manual 11.5.1, 11.5.2, 11.6.1, 11.6.2

D5Parliamentary motions

A motion to annul or modify. An approval motion where the Act requires approval before the rules take effect.

  • Manual 11.7.1 to 11.7.4
Carried in both Houses: the ministry annuls or modifies the rules, then back to B7.

D6Committee on Subordinate Legislation

Scrutiny and report. Action taken within 6 months.

  • Manual 11.8, 11.9
Rules amended on its recommendation: back to B2.
Both tracks

Access and change (both tracks)

D7Public APIs

ProposalFetch a law by URI or by date. Verify a document or its hash. Subscribe to publication events. Offline verification also works, because the signatures are self-contained.

  • AKN FRBRuri
  • G.S.R. 660(E), r.10

D8Consumers

India Code: the Legislative Department compiles statutory rules there under the Allocation of Business Rules. Ministry websites and rules databases. Legal-tech services and courts. DigiLocker as a viewer that stores the URI only.

  • Manual 11.12, 11.13, 11.15
  • India Code
  • DigiLocker URI model

D9Change after publication

Corrigendum or lawful removal. The ministry decides if a corrigendum needs its own G.S.R. number, and lays only corrected copies. The new version goes through C1 to C9. Its log entry links to the previous hash. The old version is kept, marked as superseded.

  • Manual 11.5.7
  • Example: Legislative Department corrigendum
  • EU Regulation 2024/741 (model)
